# Blind pre-opening evaluation: Litchfield Tobacco

## Scenario
Pretend the existing Litchfield Tobacco business has never operated. Evaluate the proposed site as a new tobacco retail location.

## Allowed pre-opening evidence
- Site address and parcel/zoning, licensing and legal restrictions
- Public population and age-21+ trade area demographics
- Road AADT and access/visibility, parking
- Other stores' competition, location, hours, and publicly available popularity
- Rent, NNN, opening costs, projected margins and overhead (if known)
- City-level Census/ACS indicators, with dates and sources

## Prohibited leakage
- Existing Litchfield Tobacco Google Popular Times, review count, rating or photos showing store performance
- Clover sales, transaction counts, prior revenue, employee records, inventory turnover
- Business-specific historical traffic or performance
- Knowledge of whether the actual store succeeded

## Outputs
- Site suitability: zoning/license status, 21+ demand, competition, access and anchors
- Low/base/high monthly sales **only after** independently calibrated comparable-store benchmarks and assumptions are available
- Rent/overhead break-even using explicitly sourced or user-supplied assumptions
- Confidence and missing data; comparison to actual Clover revenue only after locking forecast

## Current state
The original store_predictor.py is an input validator, not a revenue forecasting model.
No defensible revenue forecast can be issued from city population alone.
